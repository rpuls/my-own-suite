# Screenshot photo credits

The screenshots in this folder show Immich running on a demo photo library built for the purpose. The library is not a real person's: it belongs to an invented household, and the people visible in the Cervinia photographs are AI-generated faces of people who do not exist.

The remaining 305 photographs are openly licensed works retrieved through the [Openverse](https://openverse.org/) API. Their date, location and camera metadata was replaced to place them in the demo library's timeline; the images themselves are unmodified apart from cropping and rescaling.

223 of them are licensed CC BY and are credited individually below. The other 82 are CC0 or public domain and require no credit, but are listed in `.local-tools/screenshot-rigs/immich/corpus/provenance.json` all the same.
