Every My Own Suite install ends in the same place: a machine running the full platform, ready for you to create your owner account in the browser and start installing apps. The only real decision is **where that machine lives**.

## The two paths

### A cloud server you rent

A small virtual server at a hosting provider (like DigitalOcean). The provider supplies the hardware, power, and internet connection; MOS installs itself on top.

:::note[The easiest way in, for now]
Until MOS managed hosting arrives, this is the smoothest route: the machine comes ready-made, nothing in your home needs setting up, and the install is mostly waiting. If you wouldn't call yourself technical, start here.
:::

- **Good because:** no hardware to buy, reachable from anywhere immediately, running in ~15 minutes.
- **The trade:** a monthly bill (roughly the price of the subscriptions you're replacing), and your disk physically sits in a datacenter — encrypted-at-rest per your provider, but not in your house.

### Your own hardware at home

A spare computer, a mini PC, or an old laptop that becomes your family's server. You prepare a USB installer stick, boot the machine from it, and MOS installs itself.

- **Good because:** full physical ownership, no monthly fee, and this path unlocks the built-in [real domain + HTTPS setup](/docs/guides/https-domain/) for your home network.
- **The trade:** you supply the machine, and reaching your apps from outside your home network is a separate topic (MOS keeps your suite private by default rather than exposing it to the internet).

:::note[Honest early-access note]
My Own Suite is pre-1.0 and best treated as ready for evaluation and non-critical use — keep an independent backup of anything you can't afford to lose. A cloud server installs from a single hosted command — nothing to set up on your own computer first. Own hardware is the one path that still needs Git, Node.js, and Docker locally, to build the USB installer — that's why they appear in the table. Guided video walkthroughs are planned once the platform is feature-locked. The guides below spell out every step in the meantime.
:::
